Frequently Asked Questions

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept Zelle, PayPal, Venmo, and Cash App. Payment is required upon order confirmation to lock in your baking slot.

Where and when is porch pickup?

Porch pickup takes place in Victorville, CA. Our standard weekend pickup windows are Saturdays 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and Sundays 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM. Address details are provided upon order payment.

What is the best way to reheat cinnamon rolls?

Warming your roll in the microwave for 12 to 15 seconds softens the brioche dough and lets the cream cheese frosting melt right into the center!

Is Sky's Boulangerie a licensed cottage food operation?

Yes! Sky's Boulangerie operates under California Cottage Food Law guidelines with a certified Food Handler Certificate in San Bernardino County.
